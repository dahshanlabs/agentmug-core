- Truthfully referring to the AgentMug project or an unmodified official

- Saying that software is "compatible with AgentMug" or "for AgentMug."
- Linking to the project, documentation, or official services.
- Retaining copyright, license, and NOTICE attribution required by the

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement, or official
status.

Forks may exercise all rights granted by the applicable Apache-2.0 or MIT
license, but must use their own name, visual assets, signing identity, update
